About me

I’m Reyes Ling. I started Orchid Fund as a way to practise value investing and document how I research businesses, estimate what they’re worth, and revisit my decisions when new evidence comes in.

Investment philosophy

My approach starts with a simple question: what is this business worth? I look for a meaningful gap between a conservative estimate of intrinsic value and the market price. I stay within my circle of competence and pay attention to asset quality, financial strength, and the durability of earnings.

I look in three places. Some businesses sell below the worth of their tangible assets or earnings power. Others combine strong economics with a reasonable price. A third group includes sound businesses marked down after a specific event or a shift in sentiment. I’m comfortable holding cash while I wait for a price that gives me a margin of safety.
